# IPCC Assessment Report Chronology

The Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change (IPCC) has completed six assessment cycles since 1990. Each cycle produces reports from three working groups and a synthesis report.

| Cycle | Year(s) | Working Group I | Working Group II | Working Group III | Synthesis |
|-------|---------|-----------------|------------------|-------------------|-----------|
| AR1 | 1990 | [The Scientific Assessment](https://www.ipcc.ch/reports/?rp=ar1) | [The Impacts Assessment](https://www.ipcc.ch/reports/?rp=ar1) | [The Response Strategies](https://www.ipcc.ch/reports/?rp=ar1) | — |
| AR2 | 1995–1996 | [The Science of Climate Change](https://www.ipcc.ch/reports/?rp=ar2) | [Impacts, Adaptations and Mitigation](https://www.ipcc.ch/reports/?rp=ar2) | [Economic and Social Dimensions](https://www.ipcc.ch/reports/?rp=ar2) | [Second Assessment Synthesis](https://www.ipcc.ch/reports/?rp=ar2) |
| AR3 | 2001 | [The Scientific Basis](https://www.ipcc.ch/reports/?rp=ar3) | [Impacts, Adaptation, and Vulnerability](https://www.ipcc.ch/reports/?rp=ar3) | [Mitigation](https://www.ipcc.ch/reports/?rp=ar3) | [Synthesis Report](https://www.ipcc.ch/reports/?rp=ar3) |
| AR4 | 2007 | [The Physical Science Basis](https://www.ipcc.ch/reports/?rp=ar4) | [Impacts, Adaptation and Vulnerability](https://www.ipcc.ch/reports/?rp=ar4) | [Mitigation of Climate Change](https://www.ipcc.ch/reports/?rp=ar4) | [Synthesis Report](https://www.ipcc.ch/reports/?rp=ar4) |
| AR5 | 2013–2014 | [The Physical Science Basis](https://www.ipcc.ch/reports/?rp=ar5) | [Impacts, Adaptation, and Vulnerability](https://www.ipcc.ch/reports/?rp=ar5) | [Mitigation of Climate Change](https://www.ipcc.ch/reports/?rp=ar5) | [Synthesis Report](https://www.ipcc.ch/reports/?rp=ar5) |
| AR6 | 2021–2023 | [The Physical Science Basis](https://www.ipcc.ch/report/ar6/wg1/) | [Impacts, Adaptation and Vulnerability](https://www.ipcc.ch/report/ar6/wg2/) | [Mitigation of Climate Change](https://www.ipcc.ch/report/ar6/wg3/) | [Synthesis Report](https://www.ipcc.ch/report/ar6/syr/) |
